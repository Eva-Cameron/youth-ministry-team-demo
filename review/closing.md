# Close the loop

After a fix lands on `review/iteration-3`:

1. Open the staging page with Eva's owner invite cookie.
2. Click the thread's pin, and reply with one plain sentence saying what changed.
3. Press **Resolve**. The pin turns grey, and the thread moves to the Resolved filter.

Only the owner can resolve. Anyone invited can reply.
