    Our ethos

    Like Christ on the road to Emmaus, we walk alongside young people: we listen, we pray together, and we help them find their place in the Church.

    • 01Encounter

      Time to meet Christ in prayer, the sacraments and each other.

    • 02Belong

      Every young person is welcome as they are.

    • 03Grow

      Formation that is honest and rooted in the Church.

    • 04Serve

      Young people leading, serving and giving back.

    Come away feeling curious

    02Our storySince 1994

    1994 – today

    Alongside young people since 1994

    The Youth Ministry Team is the Diocese of Hexham and Newcastle's service for young people: a small staff, a team of interns and many volunteers.

    Our base

    The Emmaus Youth Village opened in 2010 in the Derwent Valley near Consett. It has five lodges with ensuite rooms, an auditorium, a dining hall and a fire pit, with space for up to 90 guests.

    Our mission

    We aim to encourage young people into a relationship with Christ and fully into the life of the Church.

    The YMT mission

    Across the diocese

    We run residential retreats for schools, The Source worship nights in parishes, the summer festivals, and trips to Flame at Wembley and the diocesan pilgrimage to Lourdes.
