What is The Source?

The Source is our recurring worship night for young people, students and anyone who wants to come. We gather to pray together through praise, worship and adoration — with testimony, music and time to be still before the Lord.

  • Doors 6:30pm · Start 7:00pm
  • Free refreshments
  • Open to Year 9+ and university students
  • Held at parishes and the Cathedral across the diocese

Watch back past nights and Tune-Ins on our YouTube channel, and follow Instagram for the next date.
