    What happens on a retreat?

    Coming to the Emmaus Village with your class: where you sleep, what you do and what to bring.

    A retreat is time away from normal life to slow down, think and pray. With your school, that means a few days at the Emmaus Youth Village in the Derwent Valley.

    Where you stay

    You sleep in ensuite lodge rooms with your friends. Meals are in the dining hall, and in the evening there is often a fire pit.

    What you do

    Our team runs the programme. There are games, small-group chats, time outside, and quiet time to think and pray. Some sessions are loud and busy; some are calm. Your teachers are there the whole time.

    You do not need to be “religious” to come. Come as you are. Your questions are welcome.

    What to bring

    Warm and waterproof clothes, trainers, a water bottle and toiletries. Your school will send the full kit list.

    Words to know

    Retreat
    Time away to slow down, reflect and pray.
    Reflection
    Thinking quietly about what something means to you.
    Small group
    A few of you with a leader, talking things through.
