Give now

Choose an amount and whether it's one-off or monthly. In production this connects to secure online giving (for example CAF Donate or the diocesan giving page) — we never ask for card or bank details on the page itself.

  • £50 — helps send a youth helper to Lourdes
  • £20/month — sponsors an intern across the year
