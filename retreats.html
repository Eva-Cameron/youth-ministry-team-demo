01 Retreat info for schools Primary & secondary

Two days off their phones. Space to breathe out.

Our residential retreats give pupils space to step off their phones, slow down and meet Christ — through the scriptures, through each other, and through a valley that makes you breathe out.

03 About your visit Pupils · Parents · Staff

Know before you go.

Everything you need, whoever you are. Open a section to read it.

For pupils

You're coming to the Emmaus Youth Village for a couple of days away with your year group. Here's the honest version:

  • You'll sleep in comfy ensuite lodge rooms with your friends
  • There's games, free time, good food and a fire pit
  • There's quiet time too — space to think, pray and switch off your phone
  • Bring: warm and waterproof clothes, trainers, a water bottle, toiletries
  • You don't need to be "religious" — just come as you are
For parents & carers

Your child is in safe, experienced hands. Here's what you need to know:

  • The whole team is trained and DBS-checked; school staff stay throughout
  • We follow the safeguarding policies of the Catholic Church in England & Wales
  • Ensuite accommodation, full board, and dietary and medical needs catered for
  • You'll receive a kit list, consent form and emergency contact details via school
  • Questions? Email admin@ymt.org or speak to your school office
For school staff

We make it easy to run a great retreat with minimal admin:

  • We provide a full programme, risk assessments and staff briefing
  • Clear staff-to-pupil ratios agreed in advance
  • Accessible facilities — tell us your needs and we'll plan around them
  • Before & after classroom resources included
  • One point of contact from enquiry to arrival
