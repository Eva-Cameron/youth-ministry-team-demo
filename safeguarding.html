01 Our commitment Diocese of Hexham & Newcastle

Care and protection, at the heart of everything.

The Youth Ministry Team is committed to the safeguarding policies and procedures of the Catholic Church in England & Wales and of the Diocese of Hexham & Newcastle. Every member of our team is trained and DBS-checked, and we work closely with schools, parishes and the diocesan safeguarding office.

If you have a concern ↘

Our commitments

  • Safer recruitment, training and DBS checks for all staff and volunteers
  • Clear codes of conduct and supervision ratios on every activity
  • Risk assessments for retreats, events and the Festival
  • A named safeguarding lead and clear reporting routes

Concerns

If you have a safeguarding concern about a child or vulnerable adult, please contact the diocesan safeguarding office or, in an emergency, the police on 999. You can also reach us at admin@ymt.org and we will help you reach the right person.
